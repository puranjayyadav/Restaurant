# Product Mission

## Pitch
**Plandit** is an AI-powered restaurant discovery and itinerary planning platform that helps foodies and travelers discover curated, "Instagram-worthy" dining experiences by providing intelligent, data-driven itineraries and insider insights.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- **The Modern Explorer**: Young adults and travelers who prioritize aesthetic and high-quality dining experiences and want an easy way to plan their days around them.
- **Local Foodies**: Residents who want to stay on top of the latest "trending" spots and discover hidden gems in their city.

### User Personas
**Alex (The Aesthetic Traveler)** (24-32)
- **Role:** Lifestyle Blogger / Frequent Traveler
- **Context:** Always looking for the most visually appealing and unique dining spots to share with their audience.
- **Pain Points:** Spending hours scouring social media (Lemon8, Instagram) for authentic recommendations and struggling to map them into a logical daily plan.
- **Goals:** Quickly generate a high-quality, efficient itinerary that hits multiple "must-see" spots without backtracking.

## The Problem

### Recommendation Overload
There are too many fragmented sources of "best of" lists (TikTok, Lemon8, Yelp, Google Maps), making it overwhelming to synthesize a coherent plan.

**Our Solution:** Centralize data from multiple platforms and use AI to transform fragmented reviews and posts into actionable itineraries.

### Inefficient Route Planning
Even when spots are identified, planning a day that makes geographical and functional sense (lunch vs dinner vs drinks) is a manual, error-prone chore.

**Our Solution:** An "Itinerary Solver" algorithm that optimizes for time, distance, and "vibe" to create the perfect day.

## Differentiators

### AI-Augmented Curation
Unlike Yelp or Google Maps which provide raw data, Plandit uses AI to extract "vibe" and "insider" insights specifically from lifestyle platforms like Lemon8.

### Smart Itinerary Solver
Plandit doesn't just list places; it solves the "traveling salesperson" problem for foodies, ensuring every stop on the itinerary makes sense in the context of the whole day.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Intelligent Itinerary Solver:** Automatically generates daily plans based on user vibes, location, and restaurant ratings.
- **Dynamic Density Heatmap:** Visualizes where the most popular and trending spots are clustered in real-time.
- **Multi-Source Data Enrichment:** Scrapers that pull from Lemon8, Yelp, and OpenTable to provide a 360-degree view of every restaurant.

### Collaboration Features
- **Creator Leaderboards:** Tracks and highlights top influencers and "insiders" who provide the best recommendations.
- **Shared Itineraries:** Users can share their generated or saved itineraries with friends.

### Advanced Features
- **AI-Powered "Insider Tidbits":** Short, actionable tips for every location (e.g., "Best for late-night vibes," "Order the truffle pasta") extracted via LLMs.
- **Visual Storyboards:** Immersive, image-heavy previews of itineraries to help users "feel" the vibe before they go.
